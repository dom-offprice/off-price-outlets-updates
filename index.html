Quick answers

Before you visit

Where are the warehouses?

Connecticut: 110 Republic Drive (left end unit), North Haven, CT 06473 — easy from New Haven, Hamden, Wallingford, and nearby towns.
Florida: 9978 Atlantic Boulevard, Jacksonville, FL 32225.

When is the next sale?

Connecticut (North Haven): Our Sep 19–20 sale is complete — thank you! We’re restocking, waiting on a few more shipments, and getting ready for the next sale. Dates will be announced on this website and on our social media.
Florida (Jacksonville): Grand opening Oct 10–11, 2026, 10 AM–4 PM both days at 9978 Atlantic Boulevard.

What deals can I expect?

Warehouse pricing on brand-name pieces — often up to 90% off retail. Typical ranges: boots, closed-toe shoes & dress shoes $20, open-toe shoes $15, sneakers $25. Clothing: sweaters from $10, short & long-sleeve tops from $5, pants, shorts, swim, dresses, and men’s polos & dress shirts from $10, denim from $15. Women’s plus sizes and men’s up to 3XL. Inventory changes every sale.

Is this a regular retail store?

No — we’re an event-style warehouse outlet. We’re open during announced sale weekends only. That’s how prices stay this low.

How do I get sale reminders?

Connecticut: follow @OffPriceOutletsCT on Instagram. Florida: follow @offpriceoutletsFL on Instagram. TikTok (@offpriceoutlets) and Facebook (Off Price Outlets) are shared—restocks, giveaways, and dates post there first.
