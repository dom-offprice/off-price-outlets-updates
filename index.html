Quick answers

Before you visit

Where is the warehouse?

We’re at 110 Republic Drive (left end unit), North Haven, CT 06473 — easy from New Haven, Hamden, Wallingford, and nearby towns.

When is the next sale?

Our August 1–2 sale is complete — thank you! We’re restocking now and will announce another sale later this August. Exact dates drop on Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook first — stay tuned.

What deals can I expect?

Warehouse pricing on brand-name pieces — often up to 90% off retail. Typical ranges: open-toe shoes $15, closed-toe shoes, dress shoes & boots $20, sneakers $25. Clothing: short & long-sleeve tops from $5, pants, shorts, swim, dresses, and men’s polos & dress shirts from $10, denim from $15. Women’s plus sizes and men’s up to 3XL. Inventory changes every sale.

Is this a regular retail store?

No — we’re an event-style warehouse outlet. We’re open during announced sale weekends only. That’s how prices stay this low.

How do I get sale reminders?

Follow @OffPriceOutletsCT on Instagram, @offpriceoutlets on TikTok, and Off Price Outlets on Facebook. Restocks, giveaways, and dates post there first.
